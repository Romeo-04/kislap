## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

Kislap helps Filipino children in Grades 1–3 practice reading aloud, one sentence at a time. Repository product truth is recorded in README.md and CONTEXT.md.

## Capabilities and constraints

Preserve the existing React/Vite application, Filipino and English interface, three stories, on-device speech recognition, local progress, stars, stickers, and offline readiness. Keep fonts and assets local. No accounts, analytics, or invented progress. Ningning is a firefly.

## Brand commitments

The user requested Candy Crush inspiration and confirmed a playful map with a calmer reading screen. Preserve Kislap, Ningning, and Filipino story content. Use the existing self-hosted Baloo 2 and Andika fonts and original mascot and sticker assets.
